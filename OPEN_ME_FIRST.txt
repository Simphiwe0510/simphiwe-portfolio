SIMPHIWE SETHOLE — OFFLINE PORTFOLIO

1. Extract the ZIP file on your laptop.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. The portfolio will open in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or your default browser.

No installation, coding program, npm command, or internet hosting is required.

Keep all folders and files together. Do not move index.html by itself because
the profile photo, CV, Jira image, Excel project, and UJ Compass prototype use
the included folders.

Offline project buttons:
- UJ Compass opens projects/UJ_Compass.html.
- Excel dashboard opens projects/Project_Selection_Dashboard.xlsx.
- Jira evidence opens assets/jira-board.png.

LinkedIn and email links require an internet connection or email app.
